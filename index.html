How the valuation works

  1. History and earnings quality. Five years of statements screened with the Beneish M-score, Sloan accruals and cash conversion.
  2. Future cash flows. Free cash flow to the firm for operating companies, excess returns for banks and insurers, dividends for REITs. Capex starts at what the company spends today and fades to its through-cycle level; a company still building capacity reinvests in line with its growth.
  3. Discount rate. CAPM cost of equity with beta regressed on two years of daily returns, blended into WACC.
  4. Discount. A ten-year explicit forecast plus terminal value, cross-checked against an exit multiple.
  5. Price. Enterprise value bridged to equity value and divided by diluted shares.
  6. Verdict. DCF, Monte Carlo, comparables and regression blended into a call and an expected alpha. Calls use a ±10% band around the price; a method that values the stock at or below zero is left out and named.

Missing debt, cash or yield data stops the model instead of defaulting to zero, so every published number traces back to a source.

Value your own stocks

The app runs on your own computer. Download the repository, then double-click run_app.bat on Windows or run_app.command on a Mac. It has five tabs:

  • Watchlist: many tickers at once, ranked by upside to fair value.
  • Portfolio: your positions, with market value, fair value and gain or loss.
  • Value one stock: every result above for any ticker, with sliders for each assumption and an AI-spending and China stress test.
  • Macro and market: rates, the yield curve, credit spreads, the VIX and jobs data from Yahoo Finance and FRED, and a 12-month recession probability that weights the Bear, Base and Bull cases.
  • How it works: the method in one page.

Sentiment (estimate revisions, analyst actions, the price trend, news tone and short interest) is shown next to each valuation and never changes it. From the command line: 

pip install -r requirements.txt
streamlit run app.py
python engine/value_ticker.py AAPL --peers MSFT GOOGL META
